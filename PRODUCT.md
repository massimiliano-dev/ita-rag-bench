# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: people who buy or decide on RAG systems in Italy (product managers, CTOs, public-administration officers) who need clear, defensible conclusions about what makes retrieval work on Italian documents, with the numbers available for anyone who wants to check them.

Secondary: engineers building RAG pipelines (full grid, per-category breakdowns, cost and latency) and NLP researchers (method, confidence intervals, reproducibility).

## Product Purpose

An end-to-end retrieval benchmark on real Italian domain documents (laws and decrees from the Gazzetta Ufficiale) that measures the whole pipeline, PDF parser → chunker → embedding → retrieval, instead of embeddings alone. The leaderboard page turns each run into conclusions a decision maker can act on. Success: a visitor leaves knowing which choices matter most for their documents, and trusts the numbers enough to cite them.

## Positioning

Existing work answers "which embedding is best for Italian" (MMTEB) or evaluates LLM knowledge (ITA-Bench, ItaEval). This benchmark measures parsing and chunking on real legal PDFs, reports the recall ceiling that parsing and chunking impose before any embedding runs, and includes questions with no answer in the corpus.

## Operating Context

- Static page generated by `itarag site` from `results/<run>/summary.json` and published with GitHub Pages; no server, no external hosts.
- First run (2026-10-26): 20 acts, 700 pages, 150 questions (135 answerable), 9 parser×chunker pairs × 2 embeddings, dense / BM25 / hybrid retrieval.
- Metrics: recall@1/5/10, MRR@10, nDCG@10, recall ceiling, per category and per document characteristic, parse and index time, query latency.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every question is a **draft** (LLM-assisted, not yet human-validated); the page must say so wherever results are shown.
- Confidence intervals exist (about ±7 points for one configuration's recall@5); differences smaller than that must not be presented as wins.
- Two documents dominate the parser comparison (a scanned 1990 law and a 2001 decree with broken font encoding); averages with and without them both exist.
- Bilingual: Italian and English, switchable on the page, same data. Technical terms (recall, chunking, embedding, parser) stay in English in both.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real results in `results/2026-10-26-mvp/summary.json`, findings in README.md "First findings".
- No users, customers, citations or endorsements exist yet; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Conclusions first, evidence one step away, never the other way round.
2. Never overstate: uncertainty and the draft status of the questions travel with every number.
3. Show the pipeline, not a single score: where recall is lost matters as much as how much.
4. Reproducible by anyone: every figure points to the run and the configuration that produced it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA; charts have text equivalents; tables are real tables with headers; works without JavaScript for reading the main conclusions.
