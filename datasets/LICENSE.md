## Third-party material inside the datasets

The `evidence` fields quote short verbatim passages of Italian normative acts as published in the
*Gazzetta Ufficiale della Repubblica Italiana* (Istituto Poligrafico e Zecca dello Stato).
Texts of official acts of the State are excluded from copyright protection by art. 5 of
Law 22 April 1941, n. 633. We nevertheless attribute them:

> Source: Gazzetta Ufficiale della Repubblica Italiana (www.gazzettaufficiale.it), original
> published versions; acts listed in `corpus/normativo/manifest.yaml`.

The quoted texts are **not** authentic copies and have no legal value; the only authentic text is the
one published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale. See `corpus/LICENSES.md` for the source terms we reviewed.
